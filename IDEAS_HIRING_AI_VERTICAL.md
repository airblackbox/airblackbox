## Two Product Wedges Identified

### 1. Multi-Jurisdiction Hiring Compliance Scanner

Expand AIR Blackbox checks beyond EU AI Act + Colorado SB 24-205 to cover the full hiring-AI regulatory stack:

**US:**
- NYC Local Law 144 (predominant vs supporting role)
- Illinois HB 3773 (ZIP-as-proxy detection, live Jan 2026)
- California FEHA Amendments (vendor liability, live Oct 2025)
- Colorado CAIA (live June 2026)
- Texas TRAIGA (live Jan 2026)

**Global:**
- South Korea AI Basic Act (extraterritorial reach, live Jan 2026)
- Singapore Workplace Fairness Act
- Brazil LGPD Art. 20 + PL 2338/2023
- Chile Law 21,719 (Dec 2026)
- Peru AI Law
- Quebec Law 25
- Ontario ESA amendments (Jan 2026)
- Australia Privacy Act reforms (Dec 2026)

**EU:**
- GDPR Art. 22 + EU AI Act Annex III high-risk hiring classification
- Emotion recognition ban
- Biometric categorization ban

### 2. Shadow AI Detection Module (NEW PRODUCT WEDGE)

The undefended attack surface: recruiters pasting resumes into ChatGPT, using sanctioned tools in unsanctioned ways. No competitor is solving this.

**Possible technical approaches:**
- Browser extension monitoring
- ATS integration scanning for AI-generated evaluation language
- Prompt-pattern detection in screening notes
- Audit log gap analysis showing decisions made without recorded review time

This is not a code scanner. This is a workplace AI usage scanner. Different ICP (HR/Compliance, not eng), different channel, different price point.
